I am working with U-NET model. To feed the data to it images should be the same patch size, meaning divisible by 256. We have two main methods for that, first one is patching and other one is padding. In patching we are doing the following math (image.shape[1/2]//patch size)*patch size. To get image into the shape of being divisible by 256 (or what ever your desirable patch size). Pixels that did not match the shape are "thrown away". In padding we do the opposite, we add black pixels/mirrors the edges of an image/takes pixels from the opposite sides of an image to the image shape unless it becomes divisable by 256. This is the process before an actual croping, cutting images into smaller parts to feed the model (i am suing patchify for that).
